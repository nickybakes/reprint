SKIV NORMAL ENEMIES

All enemies have multiple variants. 
As the run progresses or higher difficulties are selected, higher level variants are used.

1. SKIV  (id: 1, aiPattern: "skiv")
===============================================================================

  Role: Basic melee grunt. Attacks or braces for dodge.

  VARIANTS
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Key    | HP   |
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  skiv0  |  12  |   
  skiv1  |  18  |   
  skiv2  |  30  |   
  skiv3  |  42  |   
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  ACTIONS

  Strike (physical)
    skiv0-skiv2:  3-4 dmg,  weight 60
    skiv3:        4-5 dmg,  weight 70
    - Resets all player chain on hit

  Brace (defensive)
    skiv0-skiv2:  +4 Dodge,  weight 40
    skiv3:        +4 Dodge,  weight 30

  AI BEHAVIOR: Simple weighted random. No priority or block conditions.
  skiv0-skiv2 favor Strike (60/40). skiv3 favors Strike more (70/30).

2. SKIV-CUTTER  (id: 2, aiPattern: "skiv-cutter")
===============================================================================

  Role: Bleed specialist. Buffs itself with Sharpen, then hits hard with
        Cutthroat for amplified bleed damage.

  Extra tracked status(buff): sharpen

  VARIANTS
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Key             | HP   |
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  skiv_cutter0    |  10  |   
  skiv_cutter1    |  16  |   
  skiv_cutter2    |  26  |   
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  All variants share identical actions and weights.

  ACTIONS

  Cutthroat (bleed)
    All:  3-4 Bleed,  weight 60
    - Does NOT reset chain
    - Bonus damage equal to current sharpen stacks 
    - At 10+ Bleed on player -> triggers Hemorrhage (25% max HP)
    - PRIORITY: always used when sharpen > 0

  Sharpen (buff)
    All:  +2 sharpen,  weight 30
    - Next attack deals +2 bonus damage

  Double Sharp! (buff)
    All:  +4 sharpen,  weight 10
    - Next attack deals +4 bonus damage

  AI BEHAVIOR: If sharpen > 0, ALWAYS uses Cutthroat (priority condition).
  Otherwise weighted random: Cutthroat 60%, Sharpen 30%, Double Sharp! 10%.
  Pattern = buff then attack, with rare big buff.

4. SKIV-BOT  (id: 5, aiPattern: "skiv-bot")
===============================================================================

  Role: Shielded tank with self-destruct threat. Supports allies with
        shields. Creates urgency -- must be dealt with before detonation
        or shield bash becomes lethal.

  Extra tracked status: detonation/countdown
  Has SHIELD (absorbs damage before HP, does not expire)

  VARIANTS
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Key         | HP   | Shield | 
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  skiv_bot0   |  20  |   5    |   
  skiv_bot1   |  38  |   5    |   
  skiv_bot2   |  52  |   7    |   
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  ACTIONS
  

  Shield Up (shield)
    skiv_bot0:        +3 Shield,  weight 70
    skiv_bot1/bot2:   +5 Shield,  weight 70

  Armor Web (support)
    skiv_bot0:        all other enemies gain +3 Shield,  weight 30
    skiv_bot1/bot2:   all other enemies gain +5 Shield,  weight 30
    - Cooldown: 2 turns
    - BLOCKED if Skiv-Bot is the only enemy alive

  Shield Bash (physical)
    All:  damage = current Shield (consumes all),  weight 50
    - Cooldown: 2 turns
    - Resets chain on hit
    - PRIORITY (skiv_bot0): shield >= 5, OR last enemy alive with shield > 0
    - PRIORITY (skiv_bot1/bot2): shield >= 10, OR last enemy alive with shield > 0
    - BLOCKED (skiv_bot0): shield < 3
    - BLOCKED (skiv_bot1/bot2): shield < 7
    - TRIGGER EFFECT: Breaking Skiv-Bot's shield before it executes
      Shield Bash triggers Self-Destruct countdown

  Self-Destruct (physical, detonation)
    skiv_bot0:        10 dmg,  weight 0 (never chosen -- triggered only)
    skiv_bot1/bot2:   15 dmg,  weight 0 (never chosen -- triggered only)
    - 2-turn countdown timer
    - Hits ALL targets (player + enemies)
    - Destroys self on detonation

  AI BEHAVIOR: Primary loop is Shield Up (weight 70) to stack shield, then
  Shield Bash when threshold is reached (priority condition). Armor Web
  supplements allies when available. Self-Destruct is only triggered by
  the shield-break trigger effect, never chosen by weight.

  STRATEGIC NOTES:
  - Player must decide: burn through shield fast to prevent Shield Bash,
    but breaking shield triggers Self-Destruct countdown.
  - Armor Web makes Skiv-Bot dangerous in groups -- shields entire field.
  - When alone, Skiv-Bot will Shield Bash with any shield amount
    (last-enemy priority override).

5. SKIV-CYBER  (id: 3, aiPattern: "skiv-cyber")
===============================================================================

  Role: Shock specialist. Builds shock stacks to trigger Stasis, then
        punishes stunned players with Beatdown. Electro-Field adds passive
        shock on any player attack.

  Extra tracked status(trap): electroField

  VARIANTS
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Key             | HP   |
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  skiv_cyber0     |  10  |
  skiv_cyber1     |  16  |
  skiv_cyber2     |  26  |
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

  ACTIONS

  Electro-shock (shock)
    skiv_cyber0:  2-4 Shock,  weight 70
    skiv_cyber1:  3-5 Shock,  weight 70
    skiv_cyber2:  3-6 Shock,  weight 70
    - Does NOT reset chain
    - At 10+ Shock on player -> triggers Stasis

  Electro-Field (shock-field, trap)
    All:  no direct damage,  weight 30
    - While active, any player attack on this enemy inflicts 1-2 Shock
    - BLOCKED if electroField already active
    - TRIGGER EFFECT: Defeating the Skiv-Cyber while it is prepping
      Electro-Field deals 7-12 Shock to each enemy

  Beatdown (physical)
    skiv_cyber0:  1-5 dmg,  weight 100
    skiv_cyber1:  1-8 dmg,  weight 100
    skiv_cyber2:  1-8 dmg,  weight 100
    - Resets all player chain on hit
    - PRIORITY: always used when player is in Stasis
    - BLOCKED: cannot use when player is NOT in Stasis

  AI BEHAVIOR: Normal loop is Electro-shock (weight 70) to build shock
  stacks toward Stasis trigger, with Electro-Field (weight 30) for passive
  shock pressure. Once player enters Stasis, ALWAYS uses Beatdown
  (priority condition). Beatdown is completely unavailable otherwise.

  STRATEGIC NOTES:
  - Electro-Field makes attacking this enemy costly -- each hit gives
    the player 1-2 Shock.
  - Killing a Skiv-Cyber while it preps Electro-Field punishes all
    enemies with 7-12 Shock (beneficial for the player).
  - Multiple Skiv-Cybers can stack shock very quickly toward Stasis.


===============================================================================
  SHARED SYSTEMS REFERENCE
===============================================================================

  STATUS THRESHOLDS (normal enemies)
  - Bleed:  10 stacks -> Hemorrhage (deals dmg equal to 25% max of HP)
  - Shock:  10 stacks -> Stasis (skip actions for 1 turn)
  - Burn:   10 stacks -> Overheat (1-3 HP loss per turn)

  AI SELECTION FLOW
  1. Check priorityCondition on each action -- if true, use that action
  2. Remove actions where blockCondition is true
  3. Remove actions on cooldown
  4. Weighted random from remaining valid actions
  5. Fallback: first valid action

  DAMAGE TYPES
  - Physical: resets player chain on hit. undodged phys dmg reduces HP. 
  - Bleed: does NOT reset chain. deals bleed dmg. triggers hemorrhage at 10 bleed. 
  - Shock: does NOT reset chain. deals shock dmg. triggers stasis at 10 shock.
